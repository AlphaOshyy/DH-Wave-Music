Behind the sound

DH Wave Music is the independent studio and label led by Dinesh Hemantha, producer, composer, mix engineer and vocalist. Based in Makuluwa, Galle, the studio works across Sinhala rap, hip-hop, trap and contemporary pop.

From recording the first vocal to preparing a track for its audience, the focus stays on the artist, the sound and the work it takes to finish a release.
